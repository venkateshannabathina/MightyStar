## Initial findings

The model provides useful independent mouth, eye/wink and eyebrow controls. A combined expression engine has not been built.

Eight controls produced very little or no change in the front capture at weight 1 (whole-frame mean RGB difference below 0.05/255):

- 00 `Mouth_Default` — delta 0.0461.
- 11 `にやり２` — delta 0.0472.
- 16 `口横広げ` — delta 0.0259.
- 18 `Mouth_BigTongue01` — delta 0.0000.
- 21 `Eye_Default` — delta 0.0000.
- 35 `星目` — delta 0.0000.
- 37 `Brow_Default` — delta 0.0000.
- 45 `发Shadow(Ray)` — delta 0.0000.

These measurements do not prove that a control is broken. Some are neutral/default shapes, small effects or material controls that may need other conditions. Hair covers part of the brows. Three-quarter captures are oblique checks, not a full side-profile calibration.
